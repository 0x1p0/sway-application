# Sway 1.0.14

## Smoother two-finger scrolling

- Normal scrolling keeps ownership of its entire gesture. Delayed touch evidence can no longer make Sway take over halfway through or begin adjusting volume/brightness while the page scrolls.
- Edge gestures suppress their full inertia tail instead of allowing the end of a long deceleration to scroll the page after a one-second cutoff.
- New scroll beginnings, cancellations, full finger lifts, and monitoring interruptions no longer carry stale ownership into the next gesture.
- Pending touch frames are processed before the scroll-start decision. Mouse wheels and phase-less precise events remain pass-through.

Gesture regression tests cover ordinary scrolling, delayed contacts, long inertia, fast transitions, cancellation, missing phases, and interleaved mouse-wheel input. No gesture settings, palm-rejection thresholds, signing identities, or update protections were changed.

The app remains self-signed and unnotarized. Physical trackpad feel should be checked on the installed release; simulated event tests do not replace that hardware check.
