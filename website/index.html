Make it feel like you.

A few good defaults. Plenty of room to make them yours.

Gestures & edge zones

Pick Everyday, Precise, or One finger. Assign brightness, volume, or no action to each edge. Set side widths from 1–40%, sensitivity, direction, and the distance needed to activate a gesture.

One-finger mode also offers an optional top edge, from 1–30% high, with horizontal or vertical gestures and configurable actions.

Palm protection & safe testing

Sway checks starting position, finger count, movement, timing, and typing activity before accepting a gesture. Tune the intent checks and typing protection to suit your hands.

The safe test view explains which rule accepted or stopped a gesture, including contact count, distance, straightness, and duration. It leaves volume, brightness, the pointer, and haptics unchanged. Lift all fingers before trying again.

Volume, brightness & haptics

Set minimum and maximum comfort levels, configure mute behavior, or use Undo after an adjustment. Menu bar sliders are still available when gestures are paused.

Choose Soft, Standard, or Crisp native haptic patterns, taps at 2%, 5%, or 10% steps, and an optional gesture-start tap. Try the feedback in Settings. These are patterns, not a hardware strength dial; what you feel depends on your trackpad and macOS.

Appearance & everyday shortcuts

Follow the system theme or choose light or dark. Show an icon, Sway’s name, volume, or brightness in the menu bar, and customize the on-screen indicator. Native Liquid Glass is used on macOS 26+, with translucent materials on earlier versions and support for Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency.

Launch at login, exclude apps, set a pause shortcut, and choose whether Sway appears in the Dock while windows are open. Closing the last window can hide its Dock icon while menu bar controls keep running. Reopen from Spotlight if your menu bar is crowded.

Settings, help & software updates

Search the organized settings categories to find what you need. Reopen the setup guide at any time, reset gesture preferences, or open a separate Controls window.

Check for updates manually or once a day. Download and install from the app when you’re ready. Update signatures verify authenticity; they do not mean the app is Apple-notarized. Update checks contact GitHub, which can see your IP address and app version.

Small questions.
Clear answers.

Ask 0x1p0 on GitHub
Will Sway work on my Mac?

Sway supports macOS 13 Ventura and later on Apple silicon and Intel Macs, with a compatible Apple Multi-Touch trackpad. Haptics require a Force Touch trackpad. Touch support uses Apple’s private MultitouchSupport framework, so compatibility can change with OS or hardware updates.

What about external displays and audio?

Brightness controls the active built-in display; external monitor brightness via DDC isn’t supported. Volume works with audio outputs that allow software volume control. Some digital outputs have a fixed level.

Why does it need Accessibility access?

Accessibility lets Sway recognize edge swipes and prevent accepted gestures from also scrolling your page. You can use the menu bar sliders without it. The setup guide is always available in Settings → About & help.

Is it free? Does it collect my touch data?

Yes, Sway is free to download. Touch data is processed locally and isn’t stored. There’s no app account. Optional update checks contact GitHub for release information; they do not upload your gestures. The source is available on GitHub.

This website has no added analytics or advertising trackers. It is hosted on Vercel and requests public release information from GitHub to keep the download button current; those services receive normal connection information such as your IP address.

How do I update or get help?

Open Software updates in Sway to check and install a signed update. You can also download a fresh copy from GitHub Releases. For setup help, reopen Getting Started from the menu or Settings. Found something off? Let 0x1p0 know on GitHub.
